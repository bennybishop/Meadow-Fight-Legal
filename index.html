MEADOW FIGHT

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled in Meadow Fight, formerly known as Meadow Militia (Android package: com.bennybishop.meadowmilitia.game).

Who to contact

The Meadow Fight developer is the contact responsible for this app's privacy enquiries. For questions, access requests or deletion requests, email benjamin.dehant@gmail.com.

The app integrates YSO Corp GameUtils and third-party services described below. These providers may process information for their own purposes as explained in their policies. Contacting us does not require a game account.

1. Information handled by the game

CategoryExamples and purpose
Local game dataProgress, waves, in-game currency, bubble layout, selected mode, tutorial completion and sound/accessibility preferences are saved on your device to resume play.
Gameplay and usageSession information, level starts, wins, losses, retries, pauses and play duration are processed by the integrated analytics services to understand gameplay and improve the app.
Device, app and network informationApp and SDK versions, device model, operating system, memory and processor information, app/device/session identifiers, IP address and connection information can be processed by the SDKs. General location may be inferred from IP address or regional settings.
Advertising and attributionWhen advertising or attribution services are active, advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), installations, ad impressions, interactions and related measurement data may be processed, depending on service configuration and your choices.
Support correspondenceIf you email us, we receive your email address, message and information you choose to send, to answer you and handle your request.

The current game does not require account registration or ask you to provide a name, phone number or payment-card details to play. Its gameplay does not require access to your contacts, microphone, camera or precise GPS location.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to operate and save the game, measure level progression and reliability, balance and improve gameplay, respond to support and privacy requests, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations. Where enabled, advertising services use information to deliver and measure ads, attribute installations and, subject to applicable consent requirements and choices, personalise ads.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing is based on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in maintaining and improving the service where permitted, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required for advertising, tracking or analytics. Publishing this policy or continuing to play does not itself constitute consent.

3. Services and sharing

The app includes the following service integrations. The presence of a library does not mean every feature is active in every release or region. Data processing depends on enabled features, SDK configuration and privacy choices.

  • YSO Corp / GameUtils: game and session analytics, app configuration and advertising integration. See the YSO Corp Privacy Policy.
  • AppLovin MAX: ad mediation, advertising and measurement when activated. See the AppLovin Privacy Policy.
  • Tenjin: installation attribution and marketing measurement when activated. See the Tenjin Privacy Policy.
  • Meta / Facebook: app-event measurement and advertising-related services when activated. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

The integrated MAX adapters also include Adjoe, BidMachine, Bigo Ads, Pangle, DT Exchange/Fyber, InMobi, ironSource, LINE, Mintegral, MobileFuse, Moloco, Ogury, Unity Ads, Verve, Vungle/Liftoff, Yandex, YSO Network and Voodoo ADN. When an enabled network serves or measures an ad, it may receive the device, network, identifier and ad-interaction information needed for that service. Provider practices and additional partner information are described in the linked AppLovin and YSO policies.

Information may also be shared with service providers necessary to operate the app or respond to requests, or where required by law or to protect legal rights. We do not sell support correspondence. Advertising partners' use of identifiers for personalised advertising may be considered a sale or sharing under some privacy laws; you may exercise applicable opt-out rights by contacting us and using the controls described below.

4. Choices and consent

Where a consent or privacy-choice screen is presented, you can accept or decline the optional purposes offered there and revisit the available privacy controls. For help changing or withdrawing a choice, email us. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID and advertising preferences in your Android device settings. Options vary by Android version.

Restricting personalised ads does not necessarily stop all technical processing or non-personalised ads. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Resetting in-game progress is not a request to delete information already received by an SDK provider.

5. Retention and deletion

Local progress stays on your device until reset or cleared. The in-game Reset + replay tutorial option resets game progression and tutorial state but preserves sound/accessibility preferences. Android's app-storage controls can clear the app's local data. Uninstalling removes local app storage; device backup settings may affect later restoration.

To request deletion of remotely processed data, email benjamin.dehant@gmail.com with the subject “Meadow Fight data deletion”. Include the app name and enough information to locate the relevant data, such as an available app identifier or approximate usage dates. Do not send passwords or identity documents unless a proportionate verification step is specifically necessary. We may ask for limited additional information and coordinate with the relevant providers.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the enquiry, follow up and satisfy applicable legal obligations. SDK-held information follows each provider's retention rules and applicable law. YSO's policy states a maximum of 13 months for its personal-data retention, followed by anonymisation where applicable; that period does not set the retention period for other providers. Deletion may be limited where retention is legally required; genuinely anonymised statistics may remain.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising-related uses. Email the contact above to make a request. We will handle it within applicable legal time limits, subject to appropriate verification and lawful exceptions. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. Children

Children's personal information is subject to additional protections under applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided information that should not have been collected, contact us so that we can investigate and arrange appropriate deletion with the relevant provider. Do not include unnecessary information about the child in your initial message.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled. No system guarantees absolute security. Providers may process information outside your country; applicable legal safeguards and transfer arrangements are described in their privacy policies. Contact us for questions about information processed through the app.
